摘要

核心话题：官方定价 · 收费模式结构 · 付费客户规模 · 客户分层

一句话核心：招股书披露 ERP 专业版年费 5,888–16,888 元、企业版年订阅 3 万元起或按单 0.05–0.3 元/单（按量模式最低购买额 3 万元）；2024 年 SaaS 收入中年订阅占 62.1%、按单占 36.8%；超过 60% 客户为日均订单 >100 单的大型商家（门槛值，非均值）；前五大客户 2024 年合计付费 474.9 万元、仅占总收入 0.52%。

关键数据：

  • 官方定价：专业版 RMB5,888 → over RMB16,888 per year；企业版年订阅 from RMB30,000 per year；按单 RMB0.05–0.3 per order；聚胜算 3,980 → over 19,980 元/年
  • 按量模式最低购买额：RMB30,000（p.15）
  • 2024 收费结构：年订阅 544,896 千元（62.1%）／按单 323,222 千元（36.8%）／其他 9,412 千元（1.1%）
  • 客户数：45.7 千（2022）→ 62.2 千（2023）→ 88.4 千（2024）→ 92.6 千（2025H1）
  • 2024 处理订单 33.0 billion（330 亿单）；2024 SaaS 收入 877,530 千元
  • 前五大客户 2024 年付费：129.5 / 97.2 / 88.8 / 83.4 / 76.0 万元

核心矛盾：

  1. ">60% 客户日均 >100 单"是分类门槛（over 100），不是均值或典型值；招股书未给客户订单量分布，不能据此推算典型客户营收。
  2. 按单计费有 3 万元最低购买额，且只占收入 36.8%；不能用"订单量 × 单价"反推全体客户客单价。
  3. 前五大客户均为匿名代号（Customer A–P），只给成立年份、注册资本、上市状态、业务范围与城市，不点名；外部身份对应只能靠特征匹配。

跟踪要点：

  1. 客户数（时点存量）与 SaaS 收入（全年流量）不可直接相除得精确 ARPU；年内客户从 62.2 千增至 88.4 千（+42%）。
  2. 平台应用市场标价 1,000–5,888 元是 authorization fees（授权费 / marked price），不是销售合同价，切勿混用。

Jushuitan ERP – Enterprise version（p.12）

Key features: Order Management System ("OMS"), Warehousing Management System ("WMS"), Procurement Management System ("PMS"), Distribution Management System ("DMS"), On-site deployment and other value-added services and online supports. Fee model: Charged either (i) on an annual subscription basis or (ii) based on the number of orders processed on the products. For products charged on an annual subscription basis, priced from RMB30,000 per year; for products charged based on number of orders processed, priced from RMB0.05 per order to RMB0.3 per order.

Jushuitan ERP – Professional version（p.13）

Key features: OMS, PMS, DMS, Initial on-site deployment services and online support. Fee model: Charged on an annual subscription basis. Priced from RMB5,888 per year to over RMB16,888 per year.

Jushengsuan (聚胜算)（p.13）

Key features: Management reporting and analytics. Fee model: Charged on an annual subscription basis. Priced from RMB3,980 per year to over RMB19,980 per year.

All of our products are cloud-native and provided to customers as SaaS. Our products are based on browser/server architecture which can be accessed through an Internet browser without upfront localized installation.

收费模式原文（p.14）：

We typically require prepayments from our customers before we grant them access to our SaaS products. Typically, we either charge customers (i) on an annual subscription package that offers unlimited or maximum volume, or (ii) based on the volume of orders processed on the products. … We generally provide discount for subscription with longer period and larger volume. Revenue is generally recognized ratably over the contract term. Under the limited volume model, customers first purchase certain number of volume, and consumed the volume upon usage. The minimum purchase amount under this volume model is RMB30,000.（p.15）

五、平台授权费与标价（p.30–32，印刷页 208–210）

For example, the minimum price for ERP products on one major e-commerce platform is RMB1,000 per year per store, and the minimum price for ERP products on another major e-commerce platform is RMB2,000 per year per store. … we set the marked price of our ERP products on such e-commerce platforms as the respective minimum prices.（p.30） In each year/period during the Track Record Period, our ERP products are offered on application marketplaces operated by 10 e-commerce platforms, and the marked prices for our ERP products typically range from RMB1,000 to RMB5,888.（p.32）

⚠️ 此处 "marked price / authorization fees" 是平台应用市场标价与代收授权费，不是客户销售合同价；原文明确 "such authorization fees do not represent, and are in addition to, the fees paid by merchants for our products pursuant to the sales contracts"。
