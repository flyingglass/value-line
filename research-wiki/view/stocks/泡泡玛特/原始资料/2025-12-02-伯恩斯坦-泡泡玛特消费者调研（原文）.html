摘要

核心话题：用户留存 · 收藏深度 · 年支出 · LABUBU 集中度 · 支出意向与流失 · 缺货与二手 · 盲盒体验 · 渠道

一句话核心：伯恩斯坦用约 850 名中国受访者的问卷（问卷星执行）证明泡泡玛特客群以"短暂追潮者"为主而非可持续收藏者——34.04% 从未购买过、一年内才开始购买的占 38.89%、购买超两年的仅 12%，深度收藏者（拥有 20 件以上、成系列收集）仅 5.56%，因此维持 Underperform、目标价 HK$225（按 18x NTM+1 P/E）。这份报告的定位是看空方的实证弹药，与花旗同期报告结论正好相反。

关键数据：

  • 样本与执行：约 850 人，全部中国受访者，数据源 WJX.cn；评级 Underperform，目标价 HK$225（对应 18x NTM+1 P/E），公告日收盘 HK$218.80，Upside 3%
  • 人口结构：92.20% 落在 18-44 岁（25-34 岁 47.64%、35-44 岁 24.11%、18-24 岁 20.45%、45+ 岁 7.45%）；女 61.23% / 男 38.77%
  • 留存（Exhibit 3）：34.04% 从未购买过泡泡玛特；一年内首购 38.89%（<6 个月 22.81% + 6 个月-1 年 16.08%）；购买超两年仅 11.70%（≈正文 12%）
  • 收藏行为（Exhibit 4）：休闲/冲动 38.89% + 审美型 29.20% = 68.09%；深度收藏者（系统集齐、持有 20+ 件）仅 5.56%（按 5,900 万会员折算约 320 万人；若按「持有 51-100 件」口径仅约 77 万人）
  • 持有量（Exhibit 5）：60.17% 只有 1-5 件，80.15% 不足 11 件；21 件以上合计 8.87%（21-50 件 6.62% + 51-100 件 1.30% + >100 件 0.95%）
  • 频次（Exhibit 6）：每月或更频繁 16.55%（每月 12.77% + 每月多次 3.78%）；20.92% 只买过一次 + 多年内偶尔买 12.41%，合计 33.33%
  • 年支出（Exhibit 7）：68.80% 年支出低于 ¥500（<¥100 与 ¥100-199 各 21.75%、¥200-499 占 25.30%）；中段 ¥500-1,999 25.41%；≥¥2,000 仅 5.79%。按 ¥69-79 单价折算约 6-7 件/年
  • IP 首因（Exhibit 9）：LABUBU 37.94%（约为第二名的 4 倍）；无特定 IP 17.61%、MOLLY 12.29%、DIMOO 11.70%、SKULLPANDA 8.75%
  • 交叉销售（Exhibit 10 / 11）：LABUBU 之外产品购买量不足 10 件占 79.08%；零交叉购买档口径存疑——正文称 29.08% 从未买过非 LABUBU 产品，但该值与「34.04% 从未购买过泡泡玛特」矛盾，图表 0 档为 50.00%（Exhibit 11 注）；第 13 页 Exhibit 10 的 0 / 1-10 档同样存疑
  • 到访动机（第 15 页，5 分制）：浏览有趣设计 4.42 > 限量/隐藏款 3.5 > 特定 IP 发售 3.13 > 娱乐 2.59 > 社交 2.43 > 集齐系列 1.88
  • 支出意向（Exhibit 13）：增加 61.28%（其中 47.40% 只增 10-20%，仅 2.72% 增 50%+）、减少或停止 38.77%、13.24% 计划一年内完全停止
  • 增支原因（Exhibit 14）：新品更吸引 39.19% > 发现新 IP 25.68% > 集齐收藏 23.94%
  • 流失原因（Exhibit 15）：太贵/预算 29.57% vs 参与度衰减合计 ≈50%（新鲜感消退 22.87% + 对现有 IP 失去兴趣 16.77% + 盲盒疲劳 10.06%）
  • 缺货反应（Exhibit 16）：换其他 IP 29.79%、等待补货 23.05%、放弃购买 18.32%、换同 IP 其他款 15.01%、二手溢价 11.58%；生态内替代合计 44.80%
  • 二手（Exhibit 17）：75.53% 用过转售平台（闲鱼 55.44%、得物 43.26%）；溢价容忍（第 21 页）：64.54% 的买家只接受 ≤50% 溢价

核心矛盾：

  1. 留存证据不足 vs 增长仍在：一年内新客占 38.89% 是渗透率提升的证据，也是"无留存证据"的证据——本报告明确按后者解读，并把"59 万"量级的会员规模（原文为 ~59 million）与 5.56% 深度收藏者做对比，得出实际核心收藏者仅百万量级
  2. 单 IP 依赖：LABUBU 承担 37.94% 获客，二级 IP 无一接近；报告认为这意味着没有第二个突破性角色，一旦 LABUBU 热度退潮，获客会直接受冲击
  3. 缺货的"替代"≠忠诚：44.80% 在缺货时在生态内替代，其中跨 IP 替代 29.79%——报告判断这是"接受可得替代品"，而非真正的多 IP 偏好
  4. 产能扩张的两难：扩产可承接 18.32% 因缺货流失的需求，但会削弱驱动稀缺性紧迫感的机制

跟踪要点：

  1. 一年以上老客占比能否抬升（本报告 12% vs 花旗 12%——两家口径一致，说明这是共同事实）
  2. 深度收藏者占比（5.56%）与重购频次（每月+ 16.55%）的走向
  3. 非 LABUBU IP（MOLLY / SKULLPANDA / DIMOO / Twinkle Twinkle）能否把零交叉购买率降下来（正文口径 29.08% / 图表 0 档 50.00%，冲突见第 14 页备注）
  4. 年支出低于 ¥500 的 68.80% 主体能否向中段（¥500-1,999，25.41%）迁移
  5. 二手溢价容忍度（64.54% ≤50%）与转售平台参与率（75.53%）是否会随供给放量下降
  6. 报告点名的先行指标：社媒讨论量、情绪趋势、声量份额——作者认为它们先于销售数据变化

第 1 页｜封面

Pop Mart Consumer Survey: Are its core consumers sustainable collectors or transient trend chasers?

Investor perspectives on Pop Mart's customer base remains sharply divided. Some view the company's ~59 million reported members as evidence of a strong collector community capable of sustaining long-term growth. Others argue the base consists primarily of transient, trend-driven purchasers whose engagement will erode as viral momentum fades. With limited granular data available on actual behavioral patterns, we surveyed ~850 respondents across China to quantify the distribution between durable collectors and temporary participants, examining purchasing behavior, engagement depth, spending elasticity, and future intentions. Overall, the survey confirms concerns that Pop Mart's customer base is predominantly novelty-driven with temporary engagement rather than durable collector communities, and growth depends on continuous fresh releases. The key survey findings were:

The customer base exhibits shallow engagement despite viral momentum, with heavy skew toward new adopters lacking proven retention. Demographics align with expectations (92% aged 18-44, 61% female), but 39% began purchasing within the past year while only 12% had purchased beyond two years, providing minimal evidence of sustainable collector development.

Casual buyers dominate with limited ownership depth, low purchase frequency, and minimal annual spending. Casual/impulse buyers and aesthetic-driven purchasers represent 68% vs. ~6% collectors. 60% own fewer than 5 items, 80% own under 11 items, and 21% made only one purchase. Annual spending concentrates below ¥500 (69%), translating to 6-7 items yearly — confirming Pop Mart functions as occasional discretionary spending rather than high frequency routine.

LABUBU drives acquisition but creates portfolio concentration risk, with novelty-dependent traffic patterns. LABUBU drives 38% of acquisition, nearly 4x any secondary IPs. Among all respondents, 29% never bought other IPs despite exposure, indicating single-character loyalty vulnerable to abandonment. Visit motivations prioritize browsing interesting designs (4.42) over IP release tracking (3.13) or series completion (1.88), confirming traffic depends on continuous novelty rather than collection-building.

第 2 页｜投资含义摘要

Spending intentions show modest growth expectations with meaningful churn risk, driven more by engagement erosion. 61% expect to increase spending, 26% plan to decrease spending, while 13% plan complete exit within twelve months. Growth concentrates in modest 10-20% projections (47%) vs. 3% anticipating 50%+ expansion. Among those reducing spend, engagement factors (novelty worn off, losing IP interest, blind box fatigue) collectively represent ~50% of motivations vs. 30% citing price sensitivity. For those increasing, new releases drive 39%, discovering new IPs motivates 26%, and collection completion accounts for 24% — indicating expansion depends on Pop Mart's execution rather than natural engagement deepening.

Scarcity strategy generates secondary market activity but creates availability friction. When items are unavailable, 18% abandon purchases while 45% substitute within the ecosystem. Interestingly, there is heavy reliance on cross-IP substitution (29.79%) — when LABUBU isn't available, customers aren't developing authentic multi-IP preferences but rather accepting available alternatives. Blind box format retains 65% positive sentiment, though 47% find it "fun but frustrating" vs. 18% genuinely excited.

Investment Implications：We rate Pop Mart underperform.

第 3 页｜调查设计与关键发现（一）

The survey investigates five dimensions:

  • IP versus Brand Loyalty：Whether purchase decisions stem from specific character attachment or broader brand affinity to Pop Mart. This includes visit motivations to assess whether traffic is character-driven or platform-driven.
  • Scarcity Response Behavior：How customers react to product unavailability, whether they accept secondary market premiums, substitute within Pop Mart's portfolio, wait for restocks, or abandon purchases to understand both purchase urgency levels and tolerance for supply constraints.
  • Customer Segmentation and Lifetime Value：Distribution patterns across purchase frequency, volume, and annual spending intentions and underlying motivational drivers that predict retention versus attrition risk.
  • Channel Strategy and Distribution Control：Purchase channel preferences across physical retail, proprietary digital platforms, third-party e-commerce, and secondary markets to understand the strength of owned distribution vs. dependency on external platforms.

Product Ownership

[60.17% of] respondents own 1-5 items, while 19.98% own 6-10 items. Combined, 80.15% own fewer than 11 products. Mid-range collectors with 11-20 items represent 10.99%, while only 6.62% own 21-50 items. High-volume collectors are minimal, with 1.30% owning 51-100 items and 0.95% exceeding 100 items.

This concentration at low ownership levels indicates that viral attention and brand awareness have not yet translated into deep collecting behavior or significant wallet share capture. The data suggests customers are either testing the brand with minimal commitment or lacking sufficient motivation to build substantial collections. Converting low-ownership customers into more committed collectors will require increased spend to generate stronger IP attachment.

Physical stores (63.36%) and official third-party e-commerce (63.83% across Tmall/Taobao/Douyin/JD) are co-dominant distribution channels. Followed by 45.04% using WeChat stores, i.e. private traffic and PopDraw mechanism.

The 32.74% using Pop Mart's official app and 30.61% accessing Robo Stores represent meaningful but secondary channels. The less than one-third adoption indicates automated retail may serve niche convenience. This secondary market usage reinforces earlier concerns that retail availability issues have normalized resale platform dependency for a meaningful customer segment.
