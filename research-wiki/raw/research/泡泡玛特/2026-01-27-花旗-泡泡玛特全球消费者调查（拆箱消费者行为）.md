## 摘要

> 核心话题：用户结构 · 消费频次与金额 · 购买驱动 · 品牌认知 · IP 偏好 · LABUBU 专章 · 数据追踪

**一句话核心**：花旗联合自家 Research Innovation Lab 对**五国 1,501 名**泡泡玛特消费者做问卷，结论与伯恩斯坦同期报告**方向相反**——用户基数仍在扩张（**76% 首次购买在一年内**、**45% 在三个月内**），**93% 计划继续购买**、只有 **7% 明确不买**，因此维持 **Buy、目标价 HK$415**；差异的根源是**样本范围**（花旗覆盖五国 1,501 人，伯恩斯坦仅中国约 850 人）与**抽样口径**（花旗纳入英美澳日大量新客）。

**关键数据**：

- 样本：**1,501 人**，澳大利亚/中国/日本/英国/美国**各约 300 人**；评级 **Buy**，目标价 **HK$415**（现价 HK$216.20，预期回报 **92.0%**，总回报 **94.1%**；28x 26E P/E）
- 用户结构（Fig 1）：**76%** 首购在 1 年内、**45%** 在 3 个月内（中国 54%、日本 51%）；**>2 年** 老客仅 **12%**（中国 22% 为六国最高）
- 最近购买（Fig 4）：**86%** 在 3 个月内、**56%** 在过去一个月（澳 62%、日 61% 最高）

**核心矛盾**：

1. **与伯恩斯坦结论对撞**：伯恩斯坦（2025-12-02，Underperform / HK$225）称客群以"短暂追潮者"为主、留存仅 12%；花旗（2026-01-27，Buy / HK$415）称用户基数仍在快速扩张、93% 将继续购买——**同一家公司、同一季度、结论相反**

### 第 1 页｜CITI'S TAKE（首页要点）

**Pop Mart (9992.HK) — Unboxing Consumer Behavior: Citi Global Consumer Survey**

> CITI'S TAKE

We conducted a survey with 1,501 Pop Mart consumers in China, Japan, the US, UK and Australia (~300 in each country) in conjunction with Citi's Research Innovation Lab, mainly focusing on consumption pattern, purchase drivers, brand perception and IP preference. Pop Mart remains our Top Buy in China's consumer sector. We expect its breakthroughs in IP diversification, product innovation, and monetization in a wide range of fields to sustain growth in 2026. Also see our deep dive Breaking Boundaries: Playing the Long Game.

**Growing user base** — 76% of survey participants had their first PPMT purchase within 1 year, and 45% of them were even newer customers within 3 months or less. In contrast to the drop in secondary price which the market had been concerned about due to fading interest, Pop Mart's user base continues to grow globally as supply improves. Only 9% of respondents purchase from a secondhand platform.

**Purchase drivers** — 87% of respondents are somewhat or very likely to buy PPMT products in the next 3 months. New series of products, limited edition and seasonal releases are key drivers for next purchase. 34% of them shop at PPMT every month and 29% every 3 months. 26% of them plan to purchase more often. Wide range of products, better quality and lower prices would drive more frequent purchase.

**Brand perception** — 54% of respondents were very satisfied with PPMT and 39% somewhat satisfied. Gift-giving and collection/hobby are top reasons for PPMT purchase, followed by emotional satisfaction and following trends. Its character design, emotional connection and rarity/exclusivity are most valued by respondents.

**IP preference** — The top 5 IP products are LABUBU (47%), Twinkle Twinkle (27%), Skullpanda (25%), Crybaby (23%) and Molly (22%). The survey results indicate that the interest in non LABUBU IPs in overseas markets might be underestimated especially Twinkle Twinkle with ownership in the US beyond our expectation.

**LABUBU** — Nearly half of respondents were first attracted by LABUBU. Unique design, following the trend and limited-edition appeal are top reasons. New product releases, innovative designs and new color are key factors driving their next purchase. Only 2% of respondents plan no LABUBU purchase in the future with no interest anymore, becoming too expensive and worsening quality as top reasons.

### 第 24 页｜Company description / Investment strategy / Valuation / Risks

**Company description**

Pop Mart is the largest pop toy company in China and increasingly recognized globally as it expands into overseas markets. It is engaged in the entire industry chain of pop toys, from artists development to IP operation, consumer access and pop toy culture promotion. It has incubated successful proprietary & exclusive IPs, such as MOLLY, SKULLPANDA, THE MONSTERS, DIMOO, Crybaby and Hirono, and also cooperated with world-renowned IPs.

**Investment strategy**

We rate Pop Mart shares Buy. Pop Mart is China's largest pop toy company with strong IP incubation and monetization, as well as growing global footprint and recognition. The company looks well set to capture the emerging 'spiritual consumption' of young consumers (especially Gen Z) with its diverse IP characters and content-driven marketing strategy. We attribute Pop Mart's success mainly to its ability to create and operate iconic IPs to which consumers can emotionally connect. Longer term, its IP commercialization should extend beyond pop toys. Pop toys, an affordable entertainment option, is one of our most preferred segments in Consumer Discretionary.

**Valuation**

Our target price is HK$415, set at 28x 26E P/E based on a 10% premium to the stock's 4-year historical average given the company's improving growth profile and execution, implying 0.9x PEG (25-27E) or 0.8x PEG (26E). Pop Mart trades at a premium to most of its global toy and IP peers, which we attribute to its overseas expansion-led fast growth while its more established rivals record slower growth. Compared with its domestic peers, too, we believe Pop Mart deserves a premium for its leadership.

**Risks**

Our quantitative risk rating system shows a High Risk rating for the stock, but we believe it is not warranted as we believe as it is supported by qualitative factors such as execution capability and growth profile.

Key downside risks that could impede the Pop Mart stock from reaching our target price include: 1) rising competition in China's pop toy market with emerging new players; 2) global expansion disappoints; 3) inability to commercialize IPs; 4) failure to renew licenses; and 5) more stringent regulations.
